Exile Planner – Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Exile Planner ("the app") is an unofficial companion app for Path of Exile 2, made by an independent developer (DreadPyke). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: the app has no accounts and keeps your builds on your device; ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Premium is sold through Google Play.

Information kept on your device

Your builds, settings (such as the app theme) and recently viewed items are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

When you share a build, the build is written into the link itself. Nothing is uploaded to a server; anyone you send the link to can see the build.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in the app under More → Privacy choices. If you don't consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Premium removes all ads; with Premium active, the app does not load ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Premium is a monthly subscription sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the developer never sees your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active Premium subscription so it can turn Premium on, and this check happens on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

Game data and market prices

The app downloads public Path of Exile 2 market prices from GitHub Pages (github.io). Like any website, GitHub may log technical details of these requests (such as IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement. No personal information is sent with these requests.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, it opens your own email app with a message you can edit. The developer receives only what you choose to send (including your email address) and uses it only to reply and improve the app. You can ask for your emails to be deleted at any time.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking by the developer.
  • The developer does not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the UK and EU) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to its use. For information collected by Google for ads or purchases, you can manage it through your Google account and the in-app Privacy choices. If you are in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
